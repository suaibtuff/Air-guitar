Setup guide

  1. Prop the phone so the neck (nut to 4th fret) and BOTH hands are in view. Keep it bright and the phone still. The “light” chip should say ok.
  2. Press Easy calibrate. A green guide appears. Move the guitar (or phone) until the nut sits on the guide’s end with the label “thick E”/“thin e” matching your guitar’s thick and thin string sides, and the neck fills the guide up to the 4th fret. Use Rotate guide if your guitar is sideways. Then press Lock now.
  3. Wrong side? Press Swap thick/thin side. Want more precision? Use Tap calibrate (3 taps).
  4. Play only Em, G, C, D. Hold the chord until its name shows, then strum. A small wrist flick is enough, and sensitivity can go higher for lighter strums.
