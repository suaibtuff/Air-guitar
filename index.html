Setup guide

  1. Prop the phone (rear camera) so the neck and both hands are visible, steady, in good light.
  2. Press Calibrate, then tap 4 points in order: nut at the low‑E string, nut at the high‑E string, 4th fret at the high‑E string, 4th fret at the low‑E string.
  3. Hold a chord until its name appears, then strum across the strings. The strumming hand is whichever hand is not on the fretboard.
