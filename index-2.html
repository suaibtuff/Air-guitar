Setup guide

  1. Prop the phone so both hands and the guitar neck are in view. Keep it bright and still. The “light” chip should say ok.
  2. Press Start Playing, then teach Em, C, G and D one by one in section 1. Re-teach if you move the phone.
  3. Hold a chord until its name shows, then strum with the other hand. A small wrist flick is enough.
